PHY · Special RelativityThe ether question

1Frames, the Ether & Michelson–Morley

experiment ★
Definition An inertial frame is one in which Newton's first law holds — a free body moves in a straight line at constant speed. Special relativity compares only inertial frames. Classical mechanics assumes absolute time and length: t′ = t, x′ = x − vt, correct only while v ≪ c.
Newton: "time flows the same everywhere"

Michelson–Morley, 1887. At the Case School of Applied Science, Cleveland. A beam is split by a beam splitter into two equal, perpendicular arms; mirrors return them to recombine. A stationary luminous ether should make the outward and return legs unequal, so rotating the apparatus should drag the fringe pattern.

QuantityValue
Predicted fringe shift≈ 0.4 fringe
Shift actually observed< 0.01 fringe
Ether drift still alloweda few km/s at most
Memory trick The arms are perpendicular and equal, so in a real ether only the round trip differs — "parallel arm gains, perpendicular arm is dragged". The predicted shift is a fraction of one fringe, which is exactly why the test is so delicate.
Exam trap A null result removes a hypothesis; it does not prove another. Michelson won the 1907 Nobel Prize in Physics — the first American to do so — for precision optical instruments, not for relativity.
Worked example ✍ how big was the predicted shift? For an arm of length L and an ether drift v, t∥ = L/(c−v) + L/(c+v) = 2Lc/(c²−v²),   t⊥ = 2L/√(c²−v²). With v = 30 km/s, v/c = 10⁻⁴, so 1/(1−β²) ≈ 1 + β² and 1/√(1−β²) ≈ 1 + β²/2: Δt ≈ (2L/c)(β²/2) = Lβ²/c. For L = 11 m and λ = 550 nm, N = cΔt/λ ≈ 0.2 fringe, the classical prediction of the order of 0.4 fringe — and the experiment saw less than a hundredth of it.
Still the same technique Splitting one beam into two paths and comparing them is how LIGO detects gravitational waves and how stepper lithography prints features a few nanometres wide. The null result ended the ether; the interferometer never went out of fashion.

2Einstein's Two Postulates (1905)

highest-yield ★
Why it was needed Maxwell's equations give one speed for light in vacuum and no other. Galilean kinematics plus an ether predicted the same laws everywhere except in the ether frame — so ether physics would be the one exception. Einstein's 1905 paper On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies removed the exception instead of explaining it.
The two postulates ① Principle of relativity — the laws of physics are identical in every inertial frame; no closed-laboratory experiment can detect uniform absolute motion.
② Constancy of the speed of light — light in vacuum travels at c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s in every inertial frame, whatever the motion of the source or of the observer.

Worked example ✍ A pulse leaves a stationary source. Postulate 2 says every observer measures c. Postulate 1 says the laws hold in all frames, so the same equations that give the usual Doppler shift in air must give the relativistic Doppler shift in vacuum: for an observer receding at v, f′ = f√((1−β)/(1+β)).
Memory trick "The physics is the same, light is the same." Postulate 1: all observers agree on the laws. Postulate 2: all observers agree on one speed — and that one speed is what mixes space with time.
Common misconception c is not "the speed of light" in general — it is the speed in vacuum, and since 1983 the metre is defined so that c = 299 792 458 m/s exactly. In glass, water or air the speed is c/n.
One line on the general theory The general theory (1915) drops the inertial-frame restriction and treats gravitation as curvature of spacetime. That is a separate lesson: here every frame is inertial and gravity is not in the equations at all.

3The Lorentz Factor γ

MCQ staple

PHY · Special RelativityLorentz transformation

4The Lorentz Transformation

written favourite
Definition The rules that take an event from the rest frame S′ of an object to the lab frame S in which the object moves along x at speed v:
The four equations x′ = γ(x − vt)  ·  t′ = γ(t − vx/c²)  ·  y′ = y  ·  z′ = z
Inverse (swap the primes, flip the sign of v): x = γ(x′ + vt′) · t = γ(t′ + vx′/c²)
Derivation sketch ✍ why the coefficients must be γ
  1. Space and time are homogeneous, so the transform is linear: x′ = a(x − vt), t′ = b(t − k·v·x)
  2. Postulate 2 for the ray x = ct, which must also have x′ = ct′: a(c−v) = b(c − kvc) ⇒ a = b and k = 1/c²
  3. The transform for −v must be the inverse of the one for +v; with a = b = A this gives A²(1 − v²/c²) = 1, so A = γ

The interval c²t² − x² − y² − z² is the same in every inertial frame, and so is c. That is the whole reason time and length cannot both stay absolute.

5Relativistic Velocity Addition

MCQ trap
Composition If an object moves at u′ in S′ and S′ moves at v in S, then u = (u′ + v)/(1 + u′v/c²). Putting u′ = c returns u = c exactly, so c is the ceiling of every speed.
Worked example 1 ✍ Two ships head the same way, each seeing the other at 0.6c. Speed of one relative to Earth: u = (0.6c + 0.6c)/(1 + 0.36) = 0.882c — not 1.2c.
Worked example 2 ✍ Two bodies recede at 0.8c and 0.6c relative to Earth: u = (0.8c + 0.6c)/(1 + 0.8 × 0.6) = 0.946c.
Memory trick "γ in, γ out": every relativistic formula is the Newtonian one times γ (time, momentum, energy) or divided by γ (length).
Exam trap Galilean addition u = u′ + v is wrong except in the v ≪ c limit. 0.6c + 0.6c = 0.882c, never 1.2c.

PHY · Special RelativityTime dilation

6Time Dilation Δt′ = γΔt

most tested ★
Definition Two events at the same place in one inertial frame are separated there by the proper time Δt₀ — the interval read by one clock present at both. Every other inertial frame measures a longer interval: Δt′ = γΔt₀ = Δt₀/√(1 − v²/c²), and always Δt′ ≥ Δt₀.
books write it Δt = γΔt₀ or Δt′ = γΔt — check which symbol is the proper time
Derivation A: the light clock ✍ A pulse bounces between two mirrors a proper distance L₀ apart, in a clock moving at v.
  1. Own frame S′: straight up and down, so Δt₀ = 2L₀/c
  2. Lab S: each leg is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs L₀ and vΔt/2
  3. cΔt/2 = √(L₀² + v²Δt²/4) ⟹ Δt²(c² − v²) = 4L₀² ⟹ Δt = γΔt₀
Derivation B: from the Lorentz transform ✍ Both ticks are at the same place in S′, so Δx′ = 0.
  1. Δx′ = γ(Δx + vΔt′) = 0 ⟹ Δx = −vΔt′
  2. Δt′ = γ(Δt − vΔx/c²) = Δt/γ ⟹ Δt = γΔt′
Worked example 1: the muon ✍ Proper lifetime τ₀ = 2.2 μs at v = 0.8c, γ = 5/3. Δt = (5/3)(2.2 μs) = 3.67 μs; the lab path is vΔt = 0.8c × 3.67 μs = 880 m.
Worked example 2: cosmic rays ✍ At v = 0.998c, γ = 1/√0.004 = 15.8, so Δt = 15.8 × 2.2 μs = 34.8 μs and the muon covers ≈ 10.4 km — muons born high in the atmosphere reach the ground.
Memory trick "Proper time is the shortest time." One clock in one place reads the smallest interval; every other frame over-measures it, because γ ≥ 1.
Exam trap Both observers see the other's clock run slow — no contradiction, they are in different inertial frames. Only when one twin turns round do the readings differ, and then the stay-at-home twin is older.
Applications & IT link Hafele–Keating (1971): four caesium clocks flown round the world on jets came back shifted by exactly the amount γ and the difference in gravitational potential predict. GPS: satellite clocks gain ≈ 38 μs per day overall.

PHY · Special RelativityLength contraction

7Length Contraction L′ = L/γ

high-yield ★
Definition A body moving along the line joining two observers has its length measured smaller than its proper length: L = L₀√(1 − v²/c²) = L₀/γ, where L₀ is the length in the body's own rest frame. Only the component parallel to the motion contracts; every transverse dimension is unchanged.
Derivation ✍ (one line) The rod is at rest in S′, so its ends differ by Δx′ = L₀. In S both end positions must be recorded at the same instant, Δt = 0, so the time term vanishes and x′₂ − x′₁ = γ(x₂ − x₁) = γL. Hence L₀ = γL ⟹ L = L₀/γ.
Worked example ✍ A spaceship has proper length L₀ = 120 m and passes Earth at 0.8c (γ = 5/3): L = 120 × 0.6 = 72 m in the Earth frame, while its height and width are unchanged — 72 m long, H high, W wide.

Memory trick "γ divides length, γ multiplies time, and it only touches the along-motion direction." Read 1/γ straight off the γ table: at 0.8c it is 0.6, so the rod keeps 60 % of its length.
Exam trap (i) Contraction is not an optical illusion of light arriving late — it survives unlimited resolution. (ii) It is not perpendicular to the motion. (iii) The proper length L₀ is what the object itself measures. (iv) Only inertial observers can quote L = L₀/γ.
Worked example ✍ the LHC ring The Large Hadron Collider ring is 27.4 km long in the laboratory frame. The protons circling it at γ ≈ 7 500 see their own orbit as only 27.4 km / 7 500 ≈ 3.7 m of proper circumference. That is the "length-contracted universe" that makes particle physics convenient, and it is the same equation turned round.
Reading the sky Astrophysicists invert the same formula: a strongly redshifted galaxy is receding close to c, and its surface brightness, angular size and light-travel time must all be corrected with these factors. A quasar at z = 6 has been travelling since the Universe was about 1 Gyr old.

8Relativity of Simultaneity, Spacetime & the Light Cone

conceptual ★
Definition Two events simultaneous in one frame (Δt = 0) are not simultaneous in a frame moving along the line joining them: Δt′ = −γvΔx/c² ≠ 0. Their order is therefore relative. Spacetime is the single 4-dimensional entity (x, y, z, t); Minkowski (1908) showed its geometry is not Euclidean.
SeparationΔs² = c²Δt² − Δx² − Δy² − Δz²All frames must agree that
Timelike (Δx < cΔt)> 0the cause comes first
Null / lightlike (Δx = cΔt)= 0only light does this
Spacelike (Δx > cΔt)< 0order can differ; no signal connects them
Worked example ✍ Two flashes occur 1 000 km apart along a rail. An observer S at the front records them simultaneously, so Δt = 0, Δx = 1 000 km. A train passes at v = 0.6c (γ = 1.25). In the train frame Δt′ = −γvΔx/c² = −(1.25)(0.6c)(1.0 × 10⁶ m)/c² = −0.75 × 10⁶/(3 × 10⁸) s = −2.5 ms: the rear flash happens 2.5 ms earlier in the train frame.
Memory trick "Set Δt = 0 in the transform and simultaneity splits." A pair of events one frame calls simultaneous gains a gap γvΔx/c² in the other frame.
Exam trap Simultaneity is relative only along the relative motion: events side by side still agree (Δx = 0 ⟹ Δt′ = 0). The twin paradox is not a violation — that worldline is not straight.
IT link Two datacentres call different events "simultaneous" — exactly the problem above. Light cones become wall diagrams, latency budgets become Δt, and the speed of the medium (fibre, ≈ 2 × 10⁸ m/s) plays the part of c.

9Relativistic Mass, Energy & Momentum

E = mc² ★
Definition Total energy of a body of rest mass m₀ moving at v: E = γm₀c². Writing the historic relativistic mass m = γm₀, the same statement is E = mc², i.e. E = mc² + K: the total energy is the rest energy plus the kinetic energy, and K = (γ − 1)m₀c².
