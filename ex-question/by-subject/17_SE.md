## COCOMO estimation

### Q96 <sub>Q0156</sub>

*49th BCS written exam 2025 — ICT (281), question paper 2 (100 MCQ) · 2025 · MCQ · 1 mark*

Organic software of 32 KLOC: nominal development time (months) by COCOMO? (6 / 7 / 14 / 21)

- **Printed answer:** (গ) 14 months
- **Correction:** Final answer 14 is right but the working is wrong: it uses a = 3.2 (intermediate COCOMO) and miscalculates 121.76^0.38 as 5.63 (actually ≈ 6.2, giving 15.5). Basic organic COCOMO: E = 2.4×32^1.05 ≈ 91.3 PM, Tdev = 2.5×91.3^0.38 ≈ 13.9 ≈ 14 months.
- **Source:** [BCS/39_c-operator-associativity-cocomo.md](../BCS/39_c-operator-associativity-cocomo.md) · [image](../BCS/39_c-operator-associativity-cocomo.jpg)
