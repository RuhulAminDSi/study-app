Math MCQs (Q4–6 not on the page; Q8 shown with sub-parts): log2 4 + log2 8 − log4 16; 5^(x+1) − 2×5^(x−3) = 0; (2i−3j+4k)·(i+2j−k); 30° right triangle with hypotenuse 10; rectangle perimeter 24, L=3W; arc of 60° in r=30; distance (k,3)-(2,3)=5; ∫0..1 x²; P(sum 7, two dice); C(8,4).
