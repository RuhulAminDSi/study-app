## Combinations / inclusion-exclusion

### Q13 <sub>Q0175</sub>

*BPDB Assistant Engineer (CSE) (BUET) · 2023-02-24 · Written*

6 hospitals and 4 police stations; choose 4 stations for uninterrupted power supply. In how many ways if at least one hospital must be chosen?

- **Printed answer:** C(10,4) − C(6,4) = 210 − 15 = 195.
- **Correction:** Selections with no hospital are the C(4,4)=1 all-police choice, not C(6,4). Correct: 210 − 1 = 209.
