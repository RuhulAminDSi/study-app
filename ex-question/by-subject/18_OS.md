- **Printed answer:** (ক) 3

## Deadlock detection and recovery

### Q7(c) <sub>Q0051</sub>

*38th BCS Written · 2018 · Written*

What is deadlock? Describe the ways of detecting and protecting against deadlock.

- **Printed answer:** Deadlock = processes each hold a resource and wait for another. Detection: a cycle in the resource-allocation graph (sufficient only when each resource has a single instance). Recovery: kill processes or preempt resources.
- **Correction:** Incomplete. It gives only 'hold and wait' of the four Coffman conditions and covers recovery instead of protection. Prevention means denying one of mutual exclusion, hold-and-wait, no preemption or circular wait; avoidance uses the Banker's algorithm.
- **Source:** [BCS/29_circular-queue-solution-operating-system-deadlock.md](../BCS/29_circular-queue-solution-operating-system-deadlock.md) · [image](../BCS/29_circular-queue-solution-operating-system-deadlock.jpg); [BCS/24_deadlock-detection-recovery-os-hardware.md](../BCS/24_deadlock-detection-recovery-os-hardware.md) · [image](../BCS/24_deadlock-detection-recovery-os-hardware.jpg)
