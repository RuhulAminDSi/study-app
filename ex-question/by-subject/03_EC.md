## Series RLC phase angle

### Q84 <sub>Q0144</sub>

*49th BCS written exam 2025 — ICT (281), question paper 2 (100 MCQ) · 2025 · MCQ · 1 mark*

Phase difference between voltage and current in a series RLC circuit with R = 100 Ω, XL = 300 Ω, XC = 100 Ω: (0° / 45° / 60° / 90°)

- **Printed answer:** Printed key (গ) 60°, though its own working gives tan⁻¹(2) ≈ 63.43°
- **Correction:** Exact phase angle is tan⁻¹(200/100) = 63.43° (voltage leads); no option is correct — 60° is only the nearest. The question data/options are flawed.
- **Source:** [BCS/55_ieee-standards-von-neumann-rlc-phase.md](../BCS/55_ieee-standards-von-neumann-rlc-phase.md) · [image](../BCS/55_ieee-standards-von-neumann-rlc-phase.jpg)
