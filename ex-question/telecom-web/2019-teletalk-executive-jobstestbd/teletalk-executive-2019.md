### Teletalk Executive 08.11.2019

Teletalk Bangladesh Limited (TBL), Executive (Technical) and Executive (General) - Exam date: 08.11.2019, 3.00 PM to 4.30 PM - **Exam taker: not printed** - MCQ 40 questions 40 marks + Written 60 marks. Document type: exam paper (blog transcription).

**MCQ part (40 × 1).** English 1-10, Mathematics 11-20, Bangla 21-25, GK 26-30 and 39: listed only. IT and electronics items:

**31.** Which of the following errors is generated when rules of a programming language is violated?
> Printed answer: Syntax error.

**32.** The systems software that creates the final executable file is:
> Printed answer: Linker.

**33.** When the diode current is large, the bias is—-.
> Printed answer: reverse.
> Answer issue: a large diode current flows only under **forward** bias; under reverse bias only a tiny leakage current flows.

**34.** What causes holes in a intrinsic semiconductor at room temperature.
> Printed answer: Thermal energy.

**35.** The capacitance of a varactor diode increases when reverse voltage across it—-.
> Printed answer: decreases.

**36.** The reverse current in diode consists of minority carrier current and—current.
> Printed answer: forward.
> Answer issue: the reverse current is the minority-carrier (saturation) current plus the **surface-leakage** current; there is no forward current under reverse bias.

**37.** The base emitter voltage is usually—-supply voltage.
> Printed answer: less than the base.

**38.** In MICR,C stands for—?
> Printed answer: Character.

**40.** The cutoff frequency of an op amp equals the unity-gain frequency divided by …….
> Printed answer: Closed loop voltage gain.
