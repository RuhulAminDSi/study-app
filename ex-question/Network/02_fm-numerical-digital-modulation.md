### 30. Digital Modulation (Digital Data-to-Analog Signal)

Important techniques:

- **ASK (Amplitude Shift Keying):** Amplitude changes according to digital data.
- **FSK (Frequency Shift Keying):** Frequency changes.
- **PSK (Phase Shift Keying):** Phase changes.
- **QPSK (Quadrature Phase Shift Keying):** Uses four phase states.
- **QAM (Quadrature Amplitude Modulation):** Uses combinations of amplitude and phase changes.
