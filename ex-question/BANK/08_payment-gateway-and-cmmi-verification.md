**9. Verification and validation are two process areas at CMMI level 3. For both of these areas (a) provide a definition (b) a description of how you can fulfill these areas in your software testing activities.**

**Answer:**

(a) Verification and validation are two separate process areas in the Capability Maturity Model Integration (CMMI) for Development, which is a framework that describes the key elements of an effective software process. These two process areas are related to ensuring that software products meet their requirements and are fit for their intended use.

**Verification:** Verification is the process of evaluating software products or related work products to ensure that they meet specified requirements and standards.

- Reviewing requirements to ensure that they are clear, complete, and consistent
- Reviewing design and architecture documents to ensure that they are appropriate for the requirements
- Reviewing code to ensure that it is correct, efficient, and maintainable
- Conducting unit tests to ensure that individual components of the software are functioning correctly
- Conducting integration tests to ensure that the software components work together correctly
- Conducting system tests to ensure that the software meets the specified requirements
- Conducting acceptance tests to ensure that the software is fit for its intended use.
