- The whole document is a power-sector **general-knowledge** bank (installed capacity, distribution companies, Rooppur/Payra/Matarbari, system loss, tariffs, transmission voltages, basic electrical facts) for the "Non Technical Part for Engineering Job" of power-sector recruitment exams. It has **no IT / CSE questions**, so per the brief nothing is transcribed; only the section is listed below.
- Items are **not attributed to any exam**: no exam name, date or exam taker is printed for any item (the one exception is a stray "NESCO AE 18" inside a worked energy calculation, item 153). It cannot add to any exam count.
- Pages 3 onward mix two numbering runs (a short-answer table 1–27, then a restart at 1/2… on page 3 continuing to 296 on page 22). Pages 5–22 use the legacy SutonnyMJ Bangla font, which the PDF text layer returns as Latin gibberish; the page renders (checked page 7) read correctly.
- Several printed answers are dated facts (e.g. capacity 29152 MW, per-capita 602 kWh, peak 15648 MW on 15 April 2023) that the compiler himself says change over time.
