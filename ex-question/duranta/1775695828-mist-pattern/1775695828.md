- **Document type:** `suggestion` (a coaching "MIST pattern" compilation). It embeds **11 real exam papers**, each with its own printed numbered banner (organisation, post, date, marks); each is bannered below as an `exam paper`. The compilation itself never counts as an exam.

## Transcription Notes

- Each paper is a table with columns SL / Question / Solution. **The Solution column is empty throughout**, except that paper 2's last question prints a C program inline (treated as a printed answer, summarised in one line).
- "Exam Taker" is printed only for paper 8 (DMTCL, **MIST**) and paper 11 (Cadet College, **MIST**). For the other nine papers no exam taker is printed; the file name ("mist-pattern") suggests MIST but that is not recorded as fact.
- Non-IT sections (Bangla, English, Math, GK, Power Sector) are listed by name, marks and item count only. In paper 4 (SPARRSO Technical Officer-IT) the banner announces "টেকনিক্যাল-৩৫ নম্বর" but **no technical section is printed**; the only computing items are two inside "সাধারণ জ্ঞান-৫×২" (transcribed). Paper 5 (BTRC AD) has an MCQ part of which 19 stems are printed with **no options**; only the one IT stem is transcribed. Paper 6 (BTRC DAD) prints no MCQ part. Paper 8 (DMTCL) prints no non-dept MCQ part. Paper 9 (DESCO) has two technical items whose code / graph are **not printed** ("Write down the output of this program", "Find the minimum spanning tree from the given graph"). Paper 7's IP question ends "Find out the following answer:" with no sub-parts printed. Paper 11's "Round Robin" question prints no process table.
- **Figures rendered as text:** three circuit diagrams in papers 5-6 (BTRC) are described in words; their exact wiring is only partly recoverable from the drawing, as noted at each.
- The PDF text layer garbles Bangla; every Bangla line was read from the rendered pages.
- Typos kept as printed: "typed of computer network", "subnet mast", "PoSQL", "Varification", "write drown different", "High pass Band pass filer", "What I hyper threading?", "got t above marks", "snip chat", "a>==0", "\"%/d\\n\"", "printf(\"%d%d n\",a,b);", "TCP/IP protocol switch each layer", "print(\"%3d\",j);", "round around time", "decimal number 9 base number", "বাংলা-(২*৫=১০০)" (for ১০), the second DESCO "Bangla (7 Marks)" heading over an English section, `[Sparrso AE 20]` tag inside the BOF 2021 paper.
- **Duplicates already in `ex-question/`:** paper 1 (SPARRSO AE 2020) items are tagged `[SPARRSO 2020]` in `bit to exabyte/17_*`, `66_*`, `67_*`; paper 2 (BOF AE(CSE) 2021) items are tagged `[BOF AE(CSE) 2021]` in `bit to exabyte/03_*`, `08_*`, `17_*`, `26_*`, `57_*`, `60_*`, `69_*`. Papers 5-6 (BTRC AD/DAD 10-12-2021) are also the subject of the separate Duranta files `1720852216-…` and `1720853431-…` (BTRC AD/DAD technical). The other papers (BOF SAE 2021, SPARRSO TO-IT 2021, BOF AP/AME 2022, DMTCL AE(ICT) 2023, DESCO AE-IT 2022, BKSP AP 2022, Cadet College Lecturer (ICT) 2025) were not found elsewhere in `ex-question/` (grep by organisation name). Note `DESCO AE 2023` in `exam-tags.json` is a different (2023) paper.
