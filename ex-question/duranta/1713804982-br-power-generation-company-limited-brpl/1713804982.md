### BRPL Assistant Manager (ICT) 29.03.2024

BR Power Generation Company Limited (BRPL) — Exam Date: 29.03.2024 — Post Name: AM (ICT) — **Exam Taker: BUET** — Non: 60, Dept Written: 40 — time: not printed — pages 1–6. Document type: exam paper.

Written:

**1. Obtain the thevenin equivalent circuit from the following figure:**

*[Figure, as text: a 5 Ω resistor (voltage vx, + at top) on the left; from its top a 10 Ω resistor in series with a dependent voltage source 4vx (+ on the left, − on the right) leads to terminal a; a 15 Ω resistor connects a to b; the bottom wire joins the 5 Ω to b. No independent source.]*

> Printed answer: test-source method, R_th = −15/2 = −7.5 Ω. Checked: correct (V_th = 0, R_th = −7.5 Ω).

**2. If v1= 1v and v2 = 2 v, find v0 in the op amp circuit:**

*[Figure, as text: stage A — inverting amplifier, input v1 through 2 kΩ, feedback 6 kΩ, output a. Stage B — inverting amplifier, input v2 through 4 kΩ, feedback 8 kΩ, output b. Stage C — inverting summer, a through 5 kΩ and b through 15 kΩ, feedback 10 kΩ, output vo.]*

> No printed answer. (Worked: a = −3 V, b = −4 V, vo = −(2a + (2/3)b) = 26/3 ≈ 8.67 V.)

**3. You have an array with n elements Write a program in any language to find to kth smallest from an array with n elements where n >= k by any programming language you can.**

> Printed answer: C program, bubble sort then return arr[k−1].

**4. Given four table Customer, Product, Employee and Order table**
**Find the firstname of customers who order all products which Categoty = 'B' and OrderDate = '15 March 2024';**
**Find the TotalOrder as Monetary Amount which is ordered between the date 15 March 2024 and 29 March 2024**

| Customer | Product |
|---|---|
| customerID, firstname, lastName, OrderDate, OrderAmount | ProductId, OrderID, Category |
| **Employee** | **Order** |
| EmpID, EmpName, EmpBirthDate, EmpRegion, EmpMobile | OrderID, ProductID, OrderNo CustomerID, TotalOder, OrderDate |

> Printed answer: (i) join with `NOT EXISTS` over products with `Category != 'B'`; (ii) `SUM(op.Quantity * p.Price)` over an `OrderProducts` table.
> Answer issue: (i) the `NOT EXISTS` tests products *outside* category B, so it does not check "all products of category B" (relational division needs: no category-B product that the customer did not order on that date); (ii) uses `OrderProducts`, `Quantity` and `Price`, which are not in the given schema — it should be `SUM(TotalOder)` from `Order` with `OrderDate BETWEEN '2024-03-15' AND '2024-03-29'`.

**5(a) You are assigned to a subnet 199.2.1.0/24. You have to assign subnets into five floors each. Now determine the range of ip address with a subnet?**

> Printed answer: borrow 3 bits → /27; floors 199.2.1.0–31, .32–63, .64–95, .96–127, .128–159.

**5(b) 20.20.2.1/20 how many possible host in this ip address ?**

> Printed answer: 2¹² − 2 = 4094 (correct).

**6. Sum of min terms given F(A,B,C,D) = ∑(0,2,5,7,8,9,10,13,15) Now find simplest form using Karnaugh Map**

> Printed answer: F = BD + B'D' (the printed K-map leaves cell 9 as 0).
> Answer issue: minterm 9 (AB'C'D) is not covered. Correct minimum: F = BD + B'D' + AC'D (or BD + B'D' + AB'C').

**8. What is the difference between Web Application firewall and Network Firewall.**

> Printed answer: WAF filters HTTP/HTTPS at layer 7 (SQLi, XSS…); network firewall filters at layers 3–4.
