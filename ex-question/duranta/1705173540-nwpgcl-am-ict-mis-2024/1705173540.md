### NWPGCL Assistant Manager (ICT) 12/01/2024

North West Power Generation Company Limited (NWPGCL) — Post: AM (ICT) — Date: 12/01/2024 — **Centre (exam taker): BUET** — Non: 60, Dept: 40 — Time: 1 hr — pages 1–10. Document type: exam paper.

**1. Salary Range and Tax Calculation are given.**

| Salary Range | Tax |
|---|---|
| 0-250000 | 0 |
| 250001-5000000 | 10% |
| 500001-100000 | 20% |
| >10,00000 | 30% |

a. Write a program using any language to the calculate the total tax of employee salary

b. From the three employee salary find the highest tax paying employee

> Printed answer: two C programs applying the slab rates cumulatively (sample: salary 6,000,000 → tax 1,625,000; 300,000/450,000/600,000 → employee 3 highest at 45,000). Checked: the slab arithmetic is correct.

**2.**

```text
Product (pname,price,category,maker)
Purchase (buyer,seller,stock)
Company(cname,country)
Person(per-name, address, phone)
```

Here in Product table make is similar to cname, here buyer and seller in Purchase table is similar to per-name in Person table,

Draw the ER diagram of from the above information

(a) Select person name who bought American category product

(b) Find Sum of all product and Sum of All sales product according to country of origin.

> Printed answer: (a) nested `EXISTS` joining `Product.pname = Purchase.seller` with `category = "American"`; (b) `SUM(Product.price)`, `SUM(Purchase.price)` grouped by `Company.country`. No ER diagram given.
> Answer issue: (a) joins the product name to the *seller* (a person) and tests `category` for "American", which is a `Company.country` value; the product bought is the Purchase column `stock`, so the query should join `Purchase.stock = Product.pname` and `Product.maker = Company.cname` with `Company.country = 'American'` (or 'USA'). (b) uses `Purchase.price`, a column that does not exist in the given schema.

**3. Given IP address 192.168.1.50, Subnet Mask: 255.255.255.240. find the valid IP range. Also find Network address and Broadcast address.**

> Printed answer: network 192.168.1.48, broadcast 192.168.1.63, valid range 192.168.1.49 – 192.168.1.62 (correct).

**[4, unnumbered]** If You are CEO of a software company. You need to develop an ERP software from following three options (i) Buy (ii) Build (iii) Open Source Modification

a) Buy: Buy a software with cost 50 lac.
b) Building: Developed by developer cost 40 lac for easy process. 50 lac for hard process. Possibility is 30% to develop in easy process.
c) Open Source and Modification : Buy and small modifications cost 30 lac, for large modifications cost 50 lac. Possibility is 80% for large.

What way you choose and why? Explain with calculation.

> Printed answer: expected costs Buy 50, Build 0.3×40 + 0.7×50 = 47, Open source 0.2×30 + 0.8×50 = 46 lac; chooses open-source modification (arithmetic correct).

**5. Draw Bit Error Rate vs Signal to Noise Ratio curve of QPSK and BPSK .**

> Printed answer: a plot of BER vs SNR for an optical link at four link lengths. Answer issue: this does not answer the question; in AWGN, coherent BPSK and Gray-coded QPSK have the same BER per bit against Eb/N0 (Pb = Q(√(2Eb/N0))), so the two curves coincide, falling from about 10⁻¹ at 0 dB to 10⁻⁵ near 9.6 dB.
