## Transcription Notes

- Coaching **suggestion** material for Nuclear Power Plant Company Bangladesh Ltd (NPCBL) engineer posts. No exam paper, exam name or exam date is printed anywhere; nothing here counts as an exam.
- **Part 1 (pp. 1-7) is a syllabus table, not questions:** "প্রশ্ন যদি সবার একই হয় তাহলে এই সিলেবাসে পড়লেই হবে। এই সাজেশন্সটি মূল সাজেশন্স, সিএসই/আইটি, বুয়েট প্যাটার্ন — BUET Pattern- NPCBL Engineer (Software/Programming)". It lists topics under 11 numbered rows (Exam-01 C Programming/OOP; 2 HTML/CSS/JavaScript; 3 Computer Fundamentals, Architecture, Microprocessor; 4 Database; 5 Operating System; 6 Networking (the digital-logic topics — number systems, Boolean algebra, K-map, MUX/decoder, flip-flops, counters — follow inside row 6 with no row 7 printed); 8 Software Engineering; 9 Compiler/TOC/ML/AI/Big Data/Cloud; 10 Security; 11 Algorithms & Data Structures; 12 Discrete Math, Basic Electrical, Communication). It is summarised here, not transcribed as questions. p. 7 is a blank logo page.
- **Parts 2-4** are domain suggestion sheets, each headed "প্রশ্ন যদি ডোমেইন বেসড হয় তাহলে মূল সিলেবাসের সাথে এই টপিক জোর দিয়ে পড়তে হবে।": Engineer (Cyber Security) pp. 8-10, Engineer (Server) pp. 11-18, Engineer (Quality) pp. 19-21. Most of each sheet is bullet topic lists (not transcribed); only the bullets printed under "গুরুত্বপূর্ণ প্রশ্ন / Important Questions / Frequently Asked / Exam Focus / Exam Questions / Questions", the "Top 20 MCQ Exam Focus", the Server "Most Important MCQs" and the Quality "Viva-তে খুব সম্ভাব্য প্রশ্ন" lists are transcribed as suggested questions. Short printed definitions (e.g. "A server is a computer system that provides services…") are coaching notes and are not transcribed.
- Stray artefacts kept out: "Use entity references:" lines (pp. 10, 13, 15, 20) repeat names (OWASP, NIST, CISA, Ubuntu Server, Windows Server, Uptime Institute, SCADA, PLC); "Availability 𝐶𝐼𝐴" on p. 20.
- The PDF text layer garbles Bangla on pp. 1 and 8-10, 19-21; those pages were read from the rendered images.
- Questions repeated across the sheets (Firewall কী?, Virtualization কী?, SDLC কী? …) are kept as printed.
- No answers printed. No duplicates of exam papers (this is not an exam paper).
