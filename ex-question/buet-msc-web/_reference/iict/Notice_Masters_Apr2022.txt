Date: 05 June 2022 
Masters (ICT) Admission Test: April 2022 
The admission test of Masters (ICT) program will be held on 14 June (Tuesday) 2022 in ECE 
Building (West Palashi), BUET, Dhaka. First there will be a one-hour MCQ type test from 9:30 AM 
to 10:30 AM. Selected candidates from the MCQ test will be eligible for the written test which will 
take place from 3:00 PM to 4:00 PM. However, candidates who completed their undergraduate 
EEE/CSE degree from BUET need to appear for an interview on 11 June (Saturday) 2022 at Room 
611 of ECE Building – they do not need to sit for MCQ/written tests.
