Postgraduate Diploma Admission
The applications will be scrutinized by the Institute and a list of eligible candidates will be displayed in the Institute’s Notice Board and website. The candidates will have to appear in tests conducted by the Selection Committee of the institute
The admission test will be held on the date published by the Institute
On the basis of the result of the admission test, a list of preliminarily selected candidates will be published.
Admission Test
MCQ test for the PG. Dip. (ICT) program generally takes place in the month of April and/or October every year. The test will be of multiple choice type and there will be a total of 60 questions. Section-wise distribution will be as follows:
Section 1: IQ – 25 questions
Section 2: English – 10 questions
Section 3: Computer Fundamentals – 25 questions
A candidate must pass each section individually.
