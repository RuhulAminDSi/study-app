Masters Admission
The applications will be scrutinized by the Institute and a list of eligible candidates will be displayed in the Institute’s Notice Board and Website. The candidates will have to appear in tests conducted by the Selection Committee of the institute
An admission test will be held on the date published by the Institute. The test generally takes place in the month of April and/or October every year.
There will be an MCQ-type test followed by a written test.
On the basis of the result of the written test, a list of preliminarily selected candidates will be published. Candidates who will pass the written test will have to appear for a viva.
MCQ Test
One has to answer all the questions from all the topics. The duration of the test will be 45 minutes.Topics for the MCQ Test:
Programming Languages
Digital Logic Design
Communication Theory
Computer Architecture and Microprocessors
Computer Networks
Electronic Devices
Written Test (ICT)
A descriptive-type written test will be conducted for the Master’s admission. There will be 2 questions on each topic. A student will answer 6 (six) sections, i.e., 12 questions (maximum). Each question will be of 5 marks. The duration of the test will be 1 hour. The topics that will be covered are as follows:
Communication Theory
Computer Architecture and microprocessors
Computer Networks
Data Structures and Algorithms
Database Management System
Digital Logic Design
Electrical Circuits
Electronic Device
Operating systems
Programming Languages
