Date: 03 June 2023 
Masters (ICT) Admission Test: April 2023 
The admission test of Masters (ICT) program will be held at 9 am on 11 June (Sunday) 2023 in ECE 
Building (West Palashi), BUET, Dhaka. There will be an MCQ type test followed by a written test 
from 9 am to 12 noon. Candidates who will pass the written test will have to appear for a viva (the 
date will be declared later).
