For each group, there will be separate Admission Tests. Each admission test will be conducted 
in two phases in one sitting as follows. There will be a MCQ exam followed by a written exam. 
However, for each group, the scripts for the latter (i.e., written exam) will be evaluated only if 
the score in the MCQ exam is higher than or equal to a cut-off mark set by the Admission 
Committee. The decision of the Admission Committee in this regard will be final. The Final 
Merit List for each group will be determined by summing the scores of MCQ and written 
exams. The duration of the MCQ Exam will be 50 minutes and that for the written exam 
will be 1 hour. The written exam will consist of 10 questions (100 marks) and the MCQ exam 
will consist of 50 questions (50 marks). There will be negative marks in the MCQ exam: for 
each wrong answer, 0.5 marks will be deducted. Only non-programmable calculators will 
be allowed.
