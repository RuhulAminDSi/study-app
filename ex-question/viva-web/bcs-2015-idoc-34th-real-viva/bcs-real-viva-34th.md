## Transcription Notes

- The compilation has well over 100 accounts, almost all general-cadre candidates (none identified as CSE). Only the three accounts with ICT questions were excerpted; the rest was read for IT content and has none.
- Text is romanised Bangla ("Banglish") as printed; it is kept that way, not converted to Bangla script.
- Excluded on purpose: "ict act -1973" in another account is the International Crimes (Tribunals) Act 1973, not an IT law.
- Account (6) also has "technology knowledge kmn? … ki ki koro? fb acnt ase? fb te akhn ki ghotona besi choltese" — personal use of technology, counted as personal.
- Account 118 has no BCS number; its date (26/04/2015) falls in the 34th BCS viva period, but `exam` is left null.
- Non-technical questions counted, not transcribed (all accounts): .
- Subject tags in backticks follow the records addendum (`ICS` for data centre / web / cloud / banking-IT systems, `NET` for networking and security, `COM` for transmission media).
- Questions are copied verbatim, including spelling and numbering slips; nothing was added. Answers written by candidates or by the host are not transcribed.
