Nyquist Sampling Rate = The minimum sample rate that captures the "essence" of the analog information. Note that while Nyquist is appropriate for sampling, it may not capture nuances in information. **Answer:**

**Modulation** is the process of varying one or more properties of a periodic waveform, called the carrier signal, with a modulating signal that typically contains information to be transmitted.
