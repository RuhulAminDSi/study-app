**26. (a) What are the types of Agent in AI and write their tasks.** — **5**

**Answer:**

In the field of Artificial Intelligence (AI), agents are entities that perceive their environment and take actions to achieve specific goals. There are several types of agents based on their characteristics, capabilities, and tasks. Here are some common types of agents along with their tasks:

**Simple Reflex Agents:**
- **Task:** These agents operate based on a direct mapping from states to actions without considering the entire history of the environment. They take actions based solely on the current percept.

**Model-Based Reflex Agents:**
- **Task:** These agents maintain an internal model of the environment to make decisions. They use this model along with the current percept to choose actions that lead to desired states.

**Goal-Based Agents:**
- **Task:** These agents have predefined goals and make decisions based on their goals and the current state of the environment. They consider various actions and their expected outcomes to achieve their objectives.

**Utility-Based Agents:**
- **Task:** These agents evaluate the utility or desirability of different actions and choose the action that maximizes expected utility. They consider factors such as costs, risks, rewards, and preferences.

**Learning Agents:**
- **Task:** These agents can improve their performance over time by learning from experience. They use data, feedback, and algorithms to update their knowledge and decision-making processes.
