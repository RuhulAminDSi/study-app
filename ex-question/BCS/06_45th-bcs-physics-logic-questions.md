**27. (a) Mention the differences between electric field and magnetic field. Two charges of Q₁=5nc and Q₂=12nc have a distance of 7μm in vacuum. Find the force exerted Q₂ by Q₁.**

**Answer:** Difference between electric field and magnetic field are:

- Electric field is the region around a charge where electric force is experienced by another charge.
- Magnetic field is the region around a magnetic pole where a magnetic material experiences magnetic force
- A changing electric field can produce a magnetic field.
- A changing magnetic field can produce an electric field.
- Electric field can influence a electric charge and can't influence a magnetic charge.
- Magnetic field can influence a magnetic charge and can't influence an electric charge.
