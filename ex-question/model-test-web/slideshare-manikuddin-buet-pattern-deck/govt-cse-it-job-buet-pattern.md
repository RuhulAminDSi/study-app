## Transcription Notes

- Slides 2–5 are motivation. Slides 7–19 cover the non-technical part (Bangla literature and grammar, English, Bangladesh and international affairs, science and ICT, maths); they quote short past Bangla questions tagged with exams (BAERA 23, NPCBL, BTCL 23, BPDB 23, SGFL 23, APSCL-21) and give reading advice. They are outside IT scope and are not transcribed.
- Slides 20–40 give, per technical subject, two columns: topics that "came in BUET job exams" with a count of how often, and topics that "have not come yet". These are the presenter's counts and have no paper-by-paper citations, so they are summarised below as blueprint evidence, not turned into records.
