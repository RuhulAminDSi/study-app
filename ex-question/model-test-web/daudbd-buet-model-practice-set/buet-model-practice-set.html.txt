নোট: নিচের সেটটি BUET কর্তৃক নেওয়া আসল প্রশ্নপত্রের হুবহু কপি নয়—এটি BUET-স্টাইল/বাংলাদেশি টেকনিক্যাল জব এক্সাম ধাঁচে মডেল প্র্যাকটিস সেট (practice only) হিসেবে প্রস্তুত করা। পরীক্ষার প্রস্তুতিতে সাহায্যের জন্য তৈরি।
  Grammar — Right Form of Verb (Q1–Q15)
      Q1. He usually (go) to campus by bus.
Answer: goes
      Q2. The engineers (solve) the issue now.
Answer: are solving
      Q3. She (be) late yesterday.
Answer: was
      Q4. I (finish) the report before you called.
Answer: had finished
      Q5. If it rains, we (cancel) the visit.
Answer: will cancel
      Q6. He (not/know) the correct formula.
Answer: does not know
      Q7. The train (arrive) at 7 pm every day.
Answer: arrives
      Q8. We (work) here since 2020.
Answer: have been working
      Q9. The project (complete) by next week.
Answer: will be completed
      Q10. He wishes he (be) an architect.
Answer: were
      Q11. Neither Rahim nor his friends (agree) with the plan.
Answer: agree
      Q12. Each of the devices (need) calibration.
Answer: needs
      Q13. You had better (check) the wiring first.
Answer: check
      Q14. Let the students (enter) the lab.
Answer: enter
      Q15. By the time we reached, the meeting (start).
Answer: had started

      Q52. She bought __ umbrella. Answer: an
      Q53. __ sun rises in the east. Answer: The
      Q54. He is __ university student. Answer: a

  Voice Change (Q56–Q65)
      Q56. The committee approved the plan. → Answer: The plan was approved by the committee.
      Q57. They are testing the circuit. → Answer: The circuit is being tested.
      Q58. He has written a paper. → Answer: A paper has been written by him.
      Q59. The project will meet the deadline. → Answer: The deadline will be met by the project.
      Q60. The engineers solved the problem. → Answer: The problem was solved by the engineers.
      Q61. People speak Bangla here. → Answer: Bangla is spoken here.
      Q62. They have completed the survey. → Answer: The survey has been completed.
      Q63. Someone has stolen my bag. → Answer: My bag has been stolen.
      Q64. The teacher is checking the scripts. → Answer: The scripts are being checked by the teacher.
      Q65. We must follow the rules. → Answer: The rules must be followed.
  Narration (Direct/Indirect) (Q66–Q70)
      Q66. He said, “I am busy now.” → Answer: He said that he was busy then.
      Q67. She said, “We will submit tomorrow.” → Answer: She said that they would submit the next day.
      Q68. The teacher said, “Don’t be late.” → Answer: The teacher advised us not to be late.
      Q69. He asked, “Where are you going?” → Answer: He asked where I was going.
      Q70. Father said, “Work hard.” → Answer: Father advised me to work hard.
  Error Spotting (Q71–Q80)
      Q71. He do not knows the rule. → Answer: He does not know the rule.
      Q72. She going to office everyday. → Answer: She goes to the office every day.
      Q73. One of the student are absent. → Answer: One of the students is absent.
      Q74. He is more senior than me. → Answer: He is senior to me.
      Q75. The news are good. → Answer: The news is good.
      Q76. She prefers tea than coffee. → Answer: She prefers tea to coffee.
      Q77. He explained me the problem. → Answer: He explained the problem to me.
      Q78. He told that he was tired. → Answer: He said that he was tired. / He told me that…
      Q79. I look forward to see you. → Answer: I look forward to seeing you.
      Q80. The committee are divided. → Answer: The committee is divided. (BrE accepted both by context)
  Sentence Completion / Cloze (Q81–Q88)
      Q81. We must comply ___ the safety rules. Answer: with
      Q82. She is capable ___ leading the team. Answer: of
      Q83. He insisted ___ paying the bill. Answer: on
      Q84. The design is consistent ___ the standard. Answer: with
      Q85. We are short ___ time. Answer: of
      Q86. She is absent ___ illness. Answer: because of / owing to
      Q87. He is absorbed ___ work. Answer: in
      Q88. They are confident ___ success. Answer: of
  One-Word Substitution (Q89–Q92)
      Q89. A person who speaks many languages — Answer: polyglot
      Q90. A speech by one person — Answer: monologue
      Q91. Fear of confined spaces — Answer: claustrophobia
      Q92. One who loves mankind — Answer: philanthropist
  Collocations & Usage (Q93–Q96)
      Q93. ___ a decision (make/take) — Answer: make a decision
      Q94. ___ an effort (do/make) — Answer: make an effort
      Q95. ___ attention (pay/give) — Answer: pay attention
      Q96. ___ progress (do/make) — Answer: make progress
  Translation (Bn→En) (Q97–Q100)
      Q97. রেলপথ দেশের অর্থনীতির জন্য অত্যন্ত গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।
Answer: Railway is extremely important for the country’s economy.
      Q98. সময়মতো কাজ সম্পন্ন করা আমাদের দায়িত্ব।
Answer: It is our responsibility to complete the work on time.
      Q99. সড়ক নিরাপত্তা সম্পর্কে সবাইকে সচেতন হতে হবে।
Answer: Everyone must be aware of road safety.
      Q100. আধুনিক প্রযুক্তি যোগাযোগকে সহজ করেছে।
Answer: Modern technology has made communication easier.
  Quick Practice Tips
  Focus: right form of verb, voice, narration, preposition, idiom & vocabulary। Method: সময় বেঁধে প্রতিদিন ২০–২৫টি প্রশ্ন ট্রাই করুন, ভুল উত্তরগুলো নোটবুকে নিয়ম দিয়ে ঠিক করুন।
