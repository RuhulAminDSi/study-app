## Content

57. What is Network Topology? Distinguish between Bus, Ring, Tree and star topology. Discuss how the Bus topology work.
58. What is the main benefit of broadband transmission system compared to baseband? What is the attenuation of transmission media? Distinguish between twisted pair, co-axial cable and fiber optics in tabular form.
59. Differentiate between OSI Model and TCP/IP Model. Draw the diagram of 4 Layer of TCP/IP Model including the main function of each layer and related protocols. List some basic functions performed at MAC Layer.
60. Draw a diagram of LAN including network Firewall. Why is firewall important in network security? List major types of network firewalls. Differentiate between Traditional Firewall and next generation Firewall.
61. Distinguish between Symmetric Encryption and asymmetric Encryption. Give Some example of encryption algorithm. What are the difference types of ciphers in cryptography? What are the factors to be considered for cryptographic strength?
62. Write down the basic difference of the following:
    a. Public vs Private IP address
    b. L2 vs L3 Switch
63. IP address silo 172...96 and subnet mask 255.255.224.0. Find the Network address?
64. Given table:
    i. BRANCH (branch_name, branch_city, assets)
    ii. CUSTOMER (customer_name, customer_street, customer_city)
    iii. ACCOUNT (account_number, branch_name, amount)
    iv. LOAN (loan_number, branch_name, amount)
    v. DEPOSITOR (customer_name, account_number)
    vi. BORROWER (customer_name, loan_number)
    vii. EMPLOYEE (employee_name, branch_name, salary)
    a) Find the name, loan number and loan amount of all customers having a loan at the Perryridge branch.
    b) Find the names of all customers whose street includes the substring "Main".
    c) Find all customers with an account but no loan.
