### Bangladesh Bank — Assistant Maintenance Engineer (AME) — 21.09.2019 — Exam taker: BUET (as printed by the coaching banner; unverified) — Marks 150, Time 1.30 h, answer all — pages 1–5

- Non-IT: Q11 and Q12 mathematics (2 questions), Q13 Focus writing in English "Impact of information technology in banking system" (1 question). Marks not printed.

**1.** What is the distributed DBMS and describe its feature?

> Printed answer: collection of logically interrelated databases over a network; location/hardware/OS/DBMS independence, distributed transactions and query processing.

**2.** How can we test ATM in distributed banking system?

> Printed answer: list of test cases (withdraw less/more/equal to balance, simultaneous withdrawals, connection loss, invalid PIN lockout, deposit, balance check).

**3.** What is the importance of application framework in web application development?

> Printed answer: reuse, upgradability/maintenance, open source, efficiency, integration.

**4.** Differentiate between micro and macro (Monolithic) kernel. What are the sub-components of I/O manager in Windows NT?

> Printed answer: microkernel minimal services vs monolithic single image; I/O manager parts: network redirector/server, cache manager, file systems, network driver, device driver.

**5.** Write the short note on Node, Hub, Backbone, Router and Gateway.

> Printed answer: one paragraph per device.

**6.** Write the categories of computer architecture. What are the stages of DLX pipelines?

> Printed answer: system design, ISA, microarchitecture; DLX stages IF, ID, EX, MEM, WB.

**7.** Differentiate among risk, threat and vulnerability. What is SSL and TSL?

> Printed answer: risk = threat × impact, threat examples, vulnerability examples (OWASP list); SSL and TLS definitions.

**8.** Four 1 kbps connections are multiplexed together. A unit is 1 bit. Find (A) the duration of 1 bit before multiplexing (B) the transmission rate of the link (C) the duration of a time slot and (D) the duration of a frame.

> Printed answer: (A) 1 ms, (B) 4 kbps, (C) 250 µs, (D) 1 ms (checked).

**9.** How can you implement AND gate & OR gate using CMOS NAND & NOR gate?

> Printed answer: CMOS NAND2/NOR2 transistor diagrams followed by an inverter, with truth tables.

**10.** A 3-phase 12 pole alternator running at 500 rpm, supplying power to a 8 pole induction motor. If slip is 3%, what is the full load speed of the motor?

> Printed answer: f = 50 Hz, Ns = 750 rpm, Nr = 727.5 rpm (checked).
