### Bangladesh Bank — Assistant Director (General) — Preliminary MCQ, ICT items (Q71–80 of the "GK & ICT" section) — 12 November 2021 — Exam taker: Faculty of Business Studies, DU (as stated by jobstestbd; unverified) — 100 MCQ, 1 hour

**71.** Technology used to provide internet by transmitting data over wires of the telephone network is?

> Printed key: DSL [Digital Subscriber Line (DSL) ]

**72.** ‘INI’ extension refers usually to what kind of file?

> Printed key: System file

**73.** What function key does F3 stand for in most computers?

> Printed key: Find

**74.** User developed instructions for Excel are called?

> Printed key: Macros

**75.** Chips are made up of millions of tiny parts/ switches known as?

> Printed key: Transistors

**76.** The ASCII code of ‘C’ is?

> Printed key: 67

**77.** Which key can be used to display the save-as box in MS word?

> Printed key: F12

**78.** Which of the following is not an internet search engine?

> Printed key: Windows [Windows is an operating system]

**79.** Firmware is held in?

> Printed key: ROM

**80.** Which one of the following is an extension of the Video file format in computer?

> Printed key: mpg [MPEG]
